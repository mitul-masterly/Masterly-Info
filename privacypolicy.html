Privacy Policy

Sudoku Puzzle

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Masterly Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with the Sudoku Puzzle mobile application ("App", package ID com.masterly.sudokupuzzle).

In short: Sudoku Puzzle is a fully offline game. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on any server. The App has no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. All your gameplay data and any images you use with the solver stay on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not connect to our servers, and does not send any of your data to us or to third parties. The App functions entirely on your device.

The App works with the following information, all stored only on your device:

  • Game progress and settings: Your current puzzle, selected difficulty level, and in-app preferences are saved locally on your device so the App can restore your game. This information never leaves your device.
  • Camera and photo images (Sudoku Solver): If you use the solver to read a printed puzzle, the App accesses your camera or a photo you select from your gallery. The image is processed entirely on your device to recognize the digits in the puzzle. Images are not uploaded, not sent anywhere, and are not shared with us or any third party.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only on your device, to:

  • Save and restore your game progress and preferences.
  • Read the numbers in a photographed or selected Sudoku puzzle so the solver can compute the solution.
  • Provide the core gameplay and solver features of the App.

3. Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions only when you use the related feature:

  • Camera: Used so you can photograph a printed Sudoku puzzle for the solver to read. The camera is optional — you can also upload a photo or type a puzzle in manually.
  • Photos / Gallery: Used so you can select an existing photo of a puzzle for the solver to read.
  • Microphone (iOS only): This permission description is required by the camera framework on iOS. The App does not record audio.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings.

4. Storage of Information

All game progress, preferences, and any temporary image files created by the solver are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this locally stored information.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking services, and does not share information with third parties.

When you download the App, the app store you use (for example, the Google Play Store or Apple App Store) processes your transaction under its own privacy policy. We do not control and are not responsible for those stores' data practices.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information, because the App does not collect it. If you use your device's share feature from within the App, any content you choose to share is handled by the apps and services you select, under their own policies.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, the App does not profile or track children.

8. Security

Because your information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us, it remains under the protection of your device's own security measures. We encourage you to keep your device secured and up to date.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Since we do not collect or hold any of your personal information, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. You remain in full control of the locally stored data and can remove it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
