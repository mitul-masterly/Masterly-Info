1. Who we are

AG HostelMate (“the app”) is built and run by Masterly Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (“we”, “us”) for the alumni of AG Hostel. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it and how you can control it. It applies to the app on Android and iOS.

By creating an account you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: create your account, show your profile, deliver messages, run events and announcements, and send notifications.
  • To show mates near you: Nearby works out distances between your saved home or work address and those of other members.
  • To keep the community safe: admins approve new members, and review reports, blocks and warnings.
  • To keep the app reliable: fix crashes, prevent abuse and improve features.
  • To contact you about the service: for example notifications about your account, events you are invited to, or hostel announcements.

We use your information only for these purposes, because you asked us to provide the service (by creating an account) and with your consent where the law requires it.

5. Service providers

We use trusted companies to run the app. They process your information only on our instructions and only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics) and Google Cloud: store your data, sign you in, deliver notifications and report crashes. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Google Maps Platform: shows maps, and suggests addresses as you search. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google location services on your phone: turn an address into map coordinates.

Your data may be stored on Google's servers outside your country. Google protects it under its own security and data-protection commitments.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our members or the public.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't follow your live location.
  • We don't read your contacts.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account:

  • Erased: your profile, photo, contact details, addresses, devices and notification tokens, sign-in history, in-app notifications, hostel membership and your place in every chat.
  • Kept, but no longer linked to you: messages you sent stay in conversations so the chat still makes sense for others, shown as from a “Former member”. Events you organised stay for their guests, with the organiser shown as “Former member”.
  • Kept for safety: reports and warnings, which are the hostel's record of moderation decisions.

Crash reports and usage statistics are kept by Firebase for a limited time and then deleted automatically.

If an admin removes you from the hostel, you are signed out and your hostel membership ends. Your account itself stays until you delete it.

9. Deleting your account

In the app:
  1. Open AG HostelMate and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.
Your account is deleted straight away, as described in section 8. If you are the only admin of a hostel with other members, you'll be asked to make someone else an admin first.

Without the app: if you can't open the app, ask us through our contact page. Tell us the email address you signed up with. We'll confirm the request comes from you and delete the account within 30 days.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • See and correct your information: most of it is in Profile → Edit profile.
  • Delete your account and personal data (section 9).
  • Withdraw consent, for example by turning off a permission or deleting your account.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or ask how it is used.
  • Raise a complaint with us, and if you aren't satisfied, with your data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, contact us. We reply within 30 days.

11. Security

Your information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and is stored on Google Cloud with access rules, so each member can only read what this policy says they can. Admin actions are checked on our servers, not just in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think your account has been misused.

12. Age limit

The app is for hostel alumni aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll change the “Last updated” date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app before they take effect.
