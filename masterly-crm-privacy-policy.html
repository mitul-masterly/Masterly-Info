1. Who we are

Masterly CRM is an employee management app provided by Masterly Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us"). It lets employees apply for leave, managers approve it, and HR maintain employee records.

The app is for our employees. There is no public sign-up: HR creates each account. We decide how and why this personal data is used, so we are responsible for it.

2. What we collect

HR enters most of this data when creating your account. You can add or change some of it yourself, and the app creates the rest as you use it.

Type of dataWhat it includes
AccountWork email address, password (stored by our sign-in provider in protected form, never visible to us), account ID
ProfileFirst and last name, phone number, home address, date of birth
EmploymentEmployee ID, department, designation, role, reporting manager, joining date, active or inactive status
Emergency contactThe name and phone number of the person you nominate
LeaveLeave balances, dates, leave type, your reason, approval status and manager comments
Activity recordsA log of changes made by administrators and managers, including who made each change and when
We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, health records or financial details, and the app has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

The reason you write for a leave request is free text and could include health information, for example for sick leave. Please share only what your manager needs to know.

If you give us someone's details as your emergency contact, please let them know. We use those details only to reach that person in an emergency.

3. How we use it

We use your data only to run the employment relationship with you, specifically to:

  • sign you in and keep your account secure
  • maintain employee records and the company directory
  • process leave requests and keep track of balances
  • show managers who is available and show HR company-wide summaries
  • produce HR reports, and meet our legal and record-keeping duties
  • contact the person you nominated in an emergency

We process this data for employment purposes and to comply with the law. We don't use it for marketing, and we don't make automated decisions about you.

4. Who can see it

Access depends on your role in the app and is enforced by security rules on our database.

WhoWhat they can see
YouYour own profile, leave balances and leave requests
Your managerYour profile, leave balances and leave requests
HR administratorsAll employee records, leave data, reports and the activity log. They can export reports as files.

Your manager's name is shown on your profile. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, for example to a government authority.

5. Service providers and location

We use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), provided by Google, to handle sign-in and to store app data. Google processes this data on our behalf under its terms for Firebase. If you get the app through Apple, Apple handles that download.

Your data is stored on servers in the United States, so it leaves India. By using these services we rely on the safeguards in Google's terms, and we will follow any restrictions the Government of India sets on such transfers.

We don't sell your personal data or share it with anyone for advertising.

6. How we protect it

  • Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Our providers encrypt stored data.
  • Database rules limit each person to the data their role allows.
  • Administrator and manager changes are logged, and the log can't be edited from the app.
  • Accounts that are deactivated lose access to company data.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires. Please keep your password private.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you work with us. After that, we keep it only as long as employment, tax and other laws require, or as needed to resolve a dispute, and then we delete it.

Activity records are kept as a security record and may still mention your account ID after your account is deleted.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other laws that apply to you, you may:

  • ask what personal data we hold and how we use it
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected or updated
  • ask us to delete your data, unless we must keep it by law
  • nominate someone to act for you if you can't
  • raise a complaint with us, then with the Data Protection Board of India if you aren't satisfied

You can change your phone, address and emergency contact in the app under My Profile. For anything else, contact HR or use the details in section 12.

9. Deleting your account

In the app, open My Profile and tap Request Account Deletion. You can also ask using the details in section 12.

HR will delete your account, profile, leave balances and leave requests within 30 days and confirm by email. If the law requires us to keep certain records, we keep only those, for only as long as required, and we'll tell you what they are.

10. Children

Masterly CRM is for employees and isn't intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and post the new version here. We'll tell you in advance about significant changes.
